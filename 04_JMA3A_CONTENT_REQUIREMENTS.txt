======================================================================
JMA3A — ORIGINAL CONTENT PACK REQUIREMENTS
Instructions for seed content created by the coding agent
======================================================================

GENERAL
-------
All seed content must be original.
Do not scrape or reproduce proprietary decks or commercial party-game cards.

Content should feel natural for Moroccan social groups.
Default packs should work for friends, university-age groups, families,
cafés, trips, and casual gatherings without requiring explicit/18+ content.

Store content as structured data validated by schemas.
Every item requires a stable unique ID.

======================================================================
1. L'INTRUS
======================================================================

NO-WORD VARIANT:
Minimum 120 words/concepts.

Suggested categories:
- food
- Morocco
- everyday objects
- places
- jobs
- football
- transport
- school/campus
- technology
- travel
- animals
- entertainment concepts

Requirements:
- not too obscure
- easy to describe without saying exact word
- avoid several nearly identical entries in same pack
- include difficulty

CLOSE-WORD VARIANT:
Minimum 80 original related pairs.

Good pair criteria:
- related enough that clue overlap creates uncertainty
- different enough that game is solvable
- culturally understandable

Do not use examples from a commercial deck as dataset.

======================================================================
2. CHKON FINA?
======================================================================

Minimum 180 prompts.
At least 100 should have polished Darija wording.

Categories:
- friends
- funny
- personality
- everyday life
- travel
- football
- campus
- family-safe

Tone:
playful, socially revealing, not cruel.

Avoid default prompts that:
- shame appearance
- target ethnicity/religion/politics/sexual orientation/medical conditions
- encourage bullying
- pressure substance use
- ask people to reveal secrets/passwords
- humiliate one participant

Prompt quality:
- should lead to a fun vote
- should be concise
- should name "who among us" naturally
- avoid repetitive templates when possible

======================================================================
3. METTELHA
======================================================================

Minimum 250 acting prompts.

Categories:
- actions
- animals
- jobs
- objects
- Moroccan daily life
- sports
- football
- travel
- places
- emotions that can be acted
- generic entertainment concepts

Each item:
id
locale
answer
category
difficulty
optional hint

Avoid prompts that require copyrighted images/audio.

======================================================================
4. MAMNOU3
======================================================================

Minimum 180 cards.

Each card:
- target
- 3 to 5 forbidden words
- locale
- category
- difficulty

Quality criteria:
- forbidden words are genuinely tempting clues
- not impossible
- avoid target appearing inside a forbidden phrase or vice versa in a broken way
- normalize accents/case for validation
- support multilingual pack separation

The app is honor-based in V1.
Do not implement fake "AI voice detection" just to claim anti-cheat.

======================================================================
5. MISSION SIRRIYA
======================================================================

Minimum 120 missions.

Each:
id
locale
instruction
difficulty
estimatedDuration
tags
safetyFlags (normally none)

Mission design:
- subtle
- harmless
- feasible in a normal gathering
- funny when revealed
- should not require a specific venue unless tagged
- not dependent on buying something

Never include default missions involving:
- dangerous actions
- illegal acts
- driving distraction
- sexual coercion
- unwanted touching
- theft
- property damage
- passwords/private messages
- photographing someone secretly
- financial spending
- alcohol/drug consumption
- humiliation
- harassment
- medical acts

======================================================================
6. LOCALIZATION STRATEGY
======================================================================

Do not assume literal translation is good game content.

Content packs may be independently authored by locale.

Example:
A funny Darija prompt can have a culturally equivalent French version
rather than a literal translation.

Keep:
content ID
pack ID
locale
version

separate enough to update content without changing game engine.

======================================================================
7. CONTENT QA
======================================================================

Create scripts/tests for:
- exact duplicate IDs
- normalized duplicate text
- empty target/prompt
- forbidden-word count
- missing category
- invalid locale
- count minimums
- unsafe development placeholders
- accidental "TODO", "lorem ipsum", "test prompt" in production packs

Generate a developer content report such as:
- count per game
- count per locale
- count per category
- duplicate warnings
